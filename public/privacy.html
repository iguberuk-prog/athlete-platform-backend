Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

The short version: we collect what we need to build your plan, we don't sell it, we don't run ads, and you can delete everything from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

Athlete Performance ("the app", "we") gives athletes personal fueling, recovery and readiness plans. Questions about this policy: support@example.com.

What we collect

  • Account: email address, password (stored only as a secure hash by our login provider), and whether you signed up as an athlete, parent or coach.
  • Athlete profile: name, date of birth, sex, height, weight, sport, position, level, optional photo, wake-up and bedtime, typical practice time, food allergies, diet, intolerances and foods you avoid, and optional parent and emergency contacts.
  • Schedule: games, practices and other events you add.
  • Daily check-ins: sleep, energy, soreness, stress, hydration, training minutes and effort, and notes you write.
  • Apple Health (iPhone app only, optional): if you tap "Fill from Apple Health," we read last night's sleep, resting heart rate and heart-rate variability on your phone and add them to that day's check-in. We only read. We never write to Apple Health.
  • Team membership: which teams you joined with a coach's code.

We do not collect your location, contacts, advertising identifiers or browsing history, and we do not use third-party analytics or ad trackers.

How we use it

  • To build your daily targets, game-day timeline, recovery plan, grocery list, trends and reminders.
  • To keep your account secure and let you sign in on any device.
  • To show a coach the limited roster view described below, only after you join their team.

We never sell your data, never share it with advertisers or data brokers, and never use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing.

What coaches can see

Only if you enter a coach's join code. The coach sees your name, photo, jersey number, position, your readiness score and the sleep and soreness from your latest check-in, your next game, your allergies and diet (so team meals are safe), whether an injury is on file, and safety flags only: not cleared to play after a concussion, out sick with a fever, carries an EpiPen, or has an inhaler. Coaches never see your contact details, date of birth, notes, medical details or full check-in history. You can leave a team at any time and the coach loses access right away. When a player joins a team, linked parents get an email about it. The app has no coach-to-player messaging.

Clubs

If your team belongs to a club that uses the app, the club's director can see a safety roster for club players: name, age, team, allergies, EpiPen and asthma flags, and emergency contact, so staff can act fast at the field. The club's athletic trainer (a licensed health care professional) can also see medical conditions, medications, current injuries and concussion steps, and can record return-to-play clearance. Coaches see only what's described above. Team announcements, challenges, homework and sign-ups are visible to the whole team and its parents; there are no private messages between coaches and players. Leaderboards show first name and last initial only.

Family links

A profile owner can create a one-time family code to share a player's profile with a parent or with the player's own account. A linked account can see the plan and health screens and log check-ins, but cannot edit or delete the profile. Either side can remove the link at any time. Players 13 and up decide whether to link a parent by creating the code themselves.

Children

Athletes 13 and older may create their own account. Children under 13 may only use the app through a parent or guardian's account, where the parent creates and controls the child's profile. We do not knowingly let a child under 13 create an account. A parent can review, correct, export or delete a child's information at any time from the app, or by emailing us. If you believe a child under 13 created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the app, under contracts that limit their use of your data to providing the service:

  • Supabase for the database and secure sign-in.
  • Netlify for hosting the app and its servers.
  • Apple for delivering the iPhone app and notifications scheduled on your device.
  • National Weather Service and Open-Meteo for forecasts and air quality. We send only a ZIP code's map point, never who you are.
  • Open Food Facts for product information when you scan a barcode. We send only the barcode number.
  • Anthropic to read meal photos (players 13 and up, only when you send one). Photos are not stored by us.
  • Stripe for club subscriptions (clubs only; families never pay in the app).
  • Resend to send weekly reports and team notices by email to linked parents.
  • Whoop and Oura (adults only), only if you connect them. We store their access keys encrypted and delete them when you disconnect.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by our database provider. Every request is checked against your login, and each account can only reach its own data. Direct public access to the database is switched off.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can download a copy from More > Account > Download my data. You can permanently delete your account and all of its data from More > Account > Delete account. Deletion is immediate in the app and is removed from backups within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to certain uses. You can do most of this in the app. For anything else, email support@example.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Health information

The app offers general sports-nutrition education, not medical care. We are not a healthcare provider, and the information you enter is not a medical record.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
